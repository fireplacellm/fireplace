    How it answers

    Grounded, cited, and checked.

    Fireplace does not improvise. For every question it runs four steps, so the answer is traceable instead of trusted on faith.

    01 / Retrieve

    Find the passages

    Hybrid keyword and semantic search runs across your packs and pods, entirely on the device.

    02 / Ground

    Read the sources

    The best passages go to a local model through llama.cpp, which answers only from what it was given.

    03 / Cite

    Show the receipts

    Every claim carries a numbered citation, and the exact source text sits under the answer.

    04 / Verify

    Flag the gaps

    A final check marks any claim the sources do not support, so you see how grounded it really is.
